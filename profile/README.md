# DELi Clinical Systems

### Digital Ecosystem for Longitudinal Clinical Intelligence

DELi Clinical Systems LLC develops research-informed healthcare technology designed to support clinical workflows, nursing informatics, utilization management, and healthcare information systems.

Our mission is to advance connected, evidence-informed clinical technology that supports healthcare professionals and organizations.

## Our Technology Ecosystem

### Continuare HIS
Healthcare information systems designed to support clinical documentation, care coordination, and longitudinal patient information.

### Continuare UM
Utilization management technology supporting clinical evidence organization, medical necessity review, and level-of-care decision support.

### ContinuRounds
Digital clinical rounding and observation workflows.

### Continuare Go
Mobile clinical workflows and connected healthcare operations.

**Development Status:** DELi's software products are under active development. Product descriptions do not imply commercial availability.
